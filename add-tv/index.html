Connect to your home Wi-Fi

Your TV and computer need to be on the same home network for setup.

1. Connect your TV

Turn on the TV. With its remote, open Settings and look for Network, Wi-Fi, or Connection. Choose your home Wi-Fi name and enter its password if asked. Leave the TV on.

2. Connect your computer

On Windows, click the Wi-Fi icon near the clock. On Mac, open Wi-Fi from the menu bar or Control Center. Choose the same Wi-Fi name you selected on the TV.

Not sure which network to choose?

Use your normal home network, not a guest network or a phone hotspot. If a device uses an Ethernet cable, connect it to the same home router. You can usually find the Wi-Fi name and password on your router or ask the person who manages your internet.

Being able to browse the internet on both devices does not necessarily mean they are on the same network.

Get the installer

One download opens your private setup guide.

Download installer

On a phone? Open this same page on your computer to continue. You’ll use your phone again at the end.

Open the installer

    Leave the launcher open

    The setup window opens in your browser. It connects directly to the TV on your Wi-Fi.

    Need help opening it?

    Continue in the setup window

    The installer takes it from here.

    1. Choose your household and TV.
    2. Approve prompts on that TV. The guide checks compatibility and tells you what to do next.
    3. Wait for the connection check, then scan the private QR code with your phone.
    Save Family TV to your phone’s Home Screen

    iPhone / iPad: Open your private dashboard in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Choose Open as Web App if offered, then Add.

    Android: In Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then Install and create shortcut. Choose Install or Create shortcut and confirm. Some versions call it Add to Home screen.

    These instructions are also in the app under Settings.

    Existing TVs update without running this guide again. Root support depends on the model and firmware; setup stops if it cannot verify support.
