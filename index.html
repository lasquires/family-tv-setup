part of your family.

We’ll walk you through connecting one TV to your private household. Your family’s controls stay separate from everyone else’s.

This pilot needs a prepared LG TV and a computer on the same Wi-Fi. Commands run on your computer. This page never receives your keys or passwords.

Still preparing the TV?

An IP address helps us find it. Installation also needs authorized root SSH access and a supported LG model and firmware. An ordinary TV pairing prompt isn’t enough. Ask the person helping you set up Family TV to prepare access first.

You can close this page at any time. Nothing runs until you paste a command into your terminal.
