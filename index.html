What will I need to do?

LG: approve the TV pairing prompt. Samsung: follow the on-screen Developer Mode steps, sign into Samsung if it requests a certificate, enter the TV pairing code, and restart when prompted. The wizard handles the installation commands.

Does the laptop have to stay on afterward?

The installed TV agents connect directly to the dashboard. The parent’s phone can connect from home or away while the TV is awake and online. Changing the TV’s IP does not change that dashboard connection.

Already-rooted TVs can be discovered too. A TV enrolled in another household is never silently transferred. Keep the private setup folder as your recovery backup.
